## 🧬 About Me

I'm a **B.Tech Computer Science Engineering** student (3rd Year, 5th Semester) at **Sarala Birla University, Jharkhand**, focused on building reliable, production-grade software and exploring the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence** and **Cybersecurity**.

My engineering approach is built on three pillars:

- 🧠 **AI/ML Systems** — designing intelligent, data-driven applications with an emphasis on real-world usability over theoretical complexity.
- 🔐 **Security-First Development** — threat modeling, malware analysis, and secure application design informed by hands-on offensive/defensive security practice.
- ⚙️ **Full Stack Product Engineering** — shipping end-to-end products, from architecture to deployment, with a product-ownership mindset.

I learn by building — every concept I study eventually becomes a project, a report, or a working prototype.
